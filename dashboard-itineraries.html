Serengeti & Ngorongoro Circuit (8 Days)

Day 1: Arrival & Kilimanjaro Airport Meet-and-Greet

Private Land Cruiser transfer to Arusha Eco Wilderness Camp. Dinner briefing with lead ranger Dr. Ndidi.

Day 2-3: Central Serengeti Seronera River Valley

Morning and late afternoon game tracking for big cats. Sundowner cocktails overlooking Moru Kopjes.

Day 4-5: Northern Serengeti Mara River Crossings

Witness the Great Migration crossing crocodile-filled river bends. Stay at mobile luxury tented camp.

Day 6-7: Ngorongoro Crater Floor Safari

600-meter descent into the caldera. Full day tracking black rhino, flamingo flocks, and hippo pools.

Day 8: Cultural Visit & Departure Flight

Maasai village walking tour followed by private bush flight transfer to Kilimanjaro International Airport.
