Expedition Booking FAQ

How far in advance should I book?

For high-demand wildlife seasons (like the Great Migration in Tanzania) or gorilla trekking permits in Rwanda, we recommend booking 4 to 8 months in advance due to strict park permit caps.

Can you customize a private itinerary for our family?

Yes. Over 35% of our bookings are fully bespoke private charters. We customize pace, lodges, vehicles, and activities tailored specifically for multi-generational families.
