Paris, Swiss Alps & Rome Grand Tour (8 Days)

Day 1: Paris Charles de Gaulle Arrival & Chauffeur Transfer

Private Mercedes transfer to Hotel Le Meurice in central Paris. Evening welcome cocktail with lead tour director Claire Beaumont.

Day 2: Private Louvre Curator Access & Sunset Seine Cruise

VIP skip-the-line museum tour with an art historian followed by a private glass-top boat cruise along the River Seine.

Day 3-4: High-Speed TGV to Switzerland & Glacier Express

First-class high-speed rail to Zermatt. Board the legendary Glacier Express through snow-capped valleys with Matterhorn views.

Day 5-6: EuroCity Rail to Italy & Imperial Rome Arrival

Cross the Italian lakes into Milan and board the Frecciarossa bullet train to Rome. Check in to Hotel de Russie near Piazza del Popolo.

Day 7: VIP Colosseum Arena & Sistine Chapel Early Access

Direct entry to the gladiators' arena floor and exclusive early-morning access to Michelangelo's Sistine Chapel frescoes.

Day 8: Roman Piazza Stroll & Departure Airport Transfer

Morning espresso tasting at Trevi Fountain, followed by private chauffeur airport transfer to Rome Fiumicino International Airport.
