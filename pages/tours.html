Common Questions

Worldwide Travel FAQ

Answers to common booking and international travel queries.

Are international flights included in the package price?

Our packages include all intra-destination transport (high-speed bullet trains, domestic scenic flights, and private transfers). International flights to/from your home airport can be bundled on request with our negotiated airline rates.

Can I customize days or add extra hotel nights?

Yes! All our itineraries can be personalized. Whether you wish to add 3 nights on the Amalfi Coast or upgrade to a suite overlooking Mount Fuji, our travel coordinators can tailor the trip to your schedule.

What deposit is needed to lock in seasonal rates?

You can lock in current rates with a 20% down payment. The remaining balance is payable 45 days prior to your departure date.
