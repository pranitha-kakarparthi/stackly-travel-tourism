Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before joining a Stackly expedition.

What fitness level is required?

Each tour is graded from Easy (accessible to anyone comfortable walking 5km/day) to Challenging (requiring multi-day aerobic stamina with pack weight). Check individual tour difficulty indicators.

Are solo travelers welcome?

Absolutely! Roughly 40% of our guests join solo. You can either share a twin tent/room with an adventurer of the same gender or opt for a private single room supplement.

What happens if weather cancels an ascent?

Safety always takes precedence. We build reserve summit buffer days into our itineraries. If conditions remain severe, we activate pre-planned contingency cultural routes without additional cost.
