Fitness Advisory

How To Train For High Elevation

Trekking 15-20km per day with 800m of elevation gain requires structured physical preparation starting 10-12 weeks before departure.

  • Aerobic Base Building: Stair-climbing and incline treadmill training with a loaded 8kg backpack.
  • Hydration Protocols: Consuming 4 liters of electrolyte fluids daily to counter altitude dehydration.
  • Footwear Break-In: Minimum 50km walked in your boots prior to day 1 on the trail.
Alpinism Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior mountaineering or rope skills?

Most of our alpine circuits are strenuous mountain walks requiring no technical ice axe or harness skills. Technical summit options are clearly identified and include a field training clinic on day 1.

How heavy is my backpack during the trek?

On our supported treks, your main luggage (up to 14kg) is transferred ahead by vehicle or pack animals between mountain chalets. You carry only a lightweight daypack (4-6kg) with water, rain jacket, and camera.
