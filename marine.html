Sustainable Seafaring

Zero Discharge & Coral Guardianship

Pioneering green maritime standards across all our expedition catamarans.

Advanced Desalination

High-efficiency reverse osmosis fresh water makers eliminating single-use plastic water jugs across all charters.

Reef-Safe Sunscreen Mandate

Complimentary biodegradable reef-friendly zinc sunblock provided to prevent coral bleaching in sheltered lagoons.

Moorings Over Anchors

Marine FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be a certified scuba diver to join?

Not at all! Over half of our guests are active snorkelers and paddle-boarders. All snorkeling excursions are guided by marine naturalists who point out sea turtles and manta rays in calm shallow lagoons.

How are seasickness issues handled?

Our twin-hull catamarans provide superior stability compared to single-hull vessels. We also provide acupressure sea-bands and non-drowsy medications on board.
