Marine FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know how to swim or scuba dive?

No! All our catamaran excursions provide flotation vests, guided shallow-lagoon snorkeling, and glass-bottom viewing boat options. Non-swimmers can comfortably enjoy all coastal activities.

Are private airport-to-dock transfers included?

Yes, every booking includes private air-conditioned vehicle transfers from the arrival airport straight to your marina or resort dock, plus all island ferry passes.
