Our Origin

Born From A Passion For The Uncharted Wild

Stackly Travel was founded by a collective of high-altitude mountaineers, marine biologists, and wildlife rangers who recognized a void in modern tourism. Travelers were being channeled through cookie-cutter tourist bus circuits, leaving the true majesty of nature unseen.

We set out to create journeys that honor the earth: small-group expeditions that tread lightly on fragile ecosystems while providing deeply immersive encounters. Today, with operations spanning 6 continents and over 85 remote regions, we remain true to that original founding ethos.

Our Mission, Vision & Values

Eco-Conscious Future

Our Environmental & Community Pledge

Every step on the trail leaves a ripple. We ensure that ripple is positive, sustainable, and life-affirming.

  • Zero Single-Use Plastics: We provide all guests with stainless purification bottles and refillable solar stations.
  • Fair Porter & Guide Wages: 100% compliant with International Porter Protection Group (IPPG) guidelines.
  • Habitat Reforestation: For every traveler booked, we plant 25 indigenous trees in deforested mountain corridors.
