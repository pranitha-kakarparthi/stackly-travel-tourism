Anti-Poaching Support

Protecting Africa's Wildlife Heritage

We believe sustainable tourism is the single most effective barrier against illegal poaching and habitat encroachment.

  • Rhino K9 Unit Funding: $150 from each booking equips canine tracking teams in the Serengeti buffer zones.
  • Maasai Community Schools: Supporting 4 pastoral schools with clean solar water purification wells.
  • Zero Off-Road Driving: Strict adherence to park boundaries preventing habitat compaction.
Safari FAQ

Essential Safari Preparation

When is the best time for the Great Migration?

July to October offers the dramatic Mara River crossings in Northern Serengeti. January to March is calving season in Southern Serengeti with dramatic predator activity.

What luggage is allowed on bush flights?

Light aircraft require soft-sided duffel bags without hard frames, with a strict maximum limit of 15 kg (33 lbs) per passenger including camera gear.
